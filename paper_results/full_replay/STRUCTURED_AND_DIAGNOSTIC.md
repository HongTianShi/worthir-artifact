# Structured and Diagnostic Reconstruction Card

`replays/structured_v2/` and `replays/hyperlink10k/` preserve participant and
organizer zones, complete released ledgers, and native scorers. Their
candidate universes and evidence text are registered task objects; they are
not open-corpus retrieval claims.

`replays/fiqa260/` contains the released 260-query diagnostic inputs used for
the cost--quality inversion. The eight FiQA-Compression260 routes can now also
be rebuilt from the official public corpus with the bundled adapter documented
in [`FIQA260.md`](FIQA260.md). `replays/dense_and_legacy_recoverability/`
preserves the historical aggregate/split replay and its scope restrictions.

The other structured and historical surfaces support replay and contract
inspection. Rebuilding their neural or corpus-materialization routes requires
the upstream snapshots and checkpoints named by their task documentation and
remains subject to those sources' terms.
